○ A tuberculosis test certificate must be submitted when applying
○ Entering students must take at least one meal a day (the fee for at least one meal is compulsory)
○ Residents must observe the dormitory rules posted on the dormitory website; violations may result in penalty points and 
forced withdrawal

○ Application: dormitory website → application for entering students (log in with date of birth) → complete the form (attach

○ Students residing abroad submit the application through the Office of International Affairs
○ First-choice room types are assigned first; if unassigned, rooms are allocated in the order of second, third and fourth choice

4. Living Information
○ What to bring: ID card, bedding, toiletries and personal items
○ Prohibited: televisions, electric cooking appliances, flammable materials, pets, gambling devices, knives and the like
○ House rules: smoking (including e-cigarettes) is strictly prohibited inside the building; quiet hours after 22:00; entry and exit 
closed from 01:00 to 05:00
○ Early withdrawal: submit a withdrawal form on the portal system at least three days in advance and obtain the supervisor’s 
confirmation
○ Refunds: the accommodation fee is settled on a sliding scale under the dormitory rules; the meal fee is refunded for the 
remaining days less five days
○ Students who withdraw early may not move in again during the same semester or the seasonal session
○ Observe the etiquette of communal living (see the dormitory website)
○ Use power strips with earth-leakage breakers in the rooms; daisy-chaining outlets is prohibited
○ Keep the international students’ kitchen tidy, hygienic and clean after use
○ Inquiries: Dormitory Office +82-62-670-2803 / https://dorm.gwangju.ac.kr

10. Important Notes
1. Applicants may not apply to more than one program.
2. All entries on the application must be accurate; applicants bear any disadvantage arising from mistaken, omitted or incorrect entries 
or from incomplete documents.
3. Admission is cancelled if the entries differ from the facts or if a matter materially affecting the admission decision was not 
disclosed in advance.
4. If omission, falsehood or fraud in material respects is found in the documents used for the admission decision, admission is 
cancelled whether before or after enrolment; if it emerges, even while enrolled, that admission was obtained by fraudulent means, 
the admission permit is revoked.
5. Where the admission permit is revoked under item 4, tuition already paid is refunded under the University’s tuition refund rules.
6. Admission scores and evaluation details are not disclosed.
7. Applications and documents submitted are not returned, whether or not the applicant is admitted; documents needed for a visa 
application should be prepared separately.
8. Applicants who fail to submit the required documents by the deadline are excluded from the interview and rejected; if this is found 
after admission, the admission is void.
9. Applicants who submit a certificate of expected graduation must submit the graduation (degree) certificate before enrolment.
10. Once the application is complete (application fee paid), it cannot be withdrawn and the fee is not refunded.
11. Admission is cancelled if tuition is not paid within the registration period; instalment and card payment are not available in the 
first semester.
12. Applicants who wish to give up their place must notify the Office of International Affairs at least seven days before the first day 
of the semester.
13. Where an applicant cannot enter Korea because a visa is refused or a change of status is not permitted, the admission permit is 
revoked and tuition is refunded under the regulations of the University.
14. New students may not take a leave of absence in their first semester.
15. If a TOPIK level rises after the application is submitted, the score report must be submitted before the interview for it to count 
towards the scholarship.
16. For notarized translation, apostille and consular authentication, enquire at the issuing authority concerned.
17. Personal data collected is used only for the admission process and is deleted after a set period (the data of admitted and 
enrolled students is used for the academic affairs of this graduate school).
18. Contact details must be accurate and reachable throughout the admission period; applicants bear any disadvantage from not 
receiving notices because of incorrect details.
19. Where the Korean text of these guidelines and a translation differ, the Korean text prevails.
20. Matters not specified in these guidelines follow the rules set by the Graduate School Committee; all matters after admission follow 
the regulations of this graduate school.
21. Inquiries: 062-670-2855 (Chinese), 2779 (Vietnamese), 2569 (Mongolian and other countries), 2858 (English)
